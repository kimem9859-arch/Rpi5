## 3. optimizer=auto
- 규칙(v8.4.171 trainer.py 1175–1177 · 324): iterations = ceil(N / max(batch, nbs=64)) × epochs · >10,000 → MuSGD(lr 0.01, m 0.9) · 이하 → AdamW(lr = round(0.002×5/(4+nc),6), m 0.9) · lr0·momentum 무시 · warmup_bias_lr=0 · 시작 때 한 번 선택.
- #2969(2023-06-04) 첫 auto: >6,000 SGD / 이하 NAdam(0.001) → #3037(8.0.114, 2023-06-06) AdamW + lr_fit · 문턱 10,000 · 기본 auto.
- glenn-jocher #3360 (2023-06-24): "I trained about 200 models on 4 datasets and noticed that AdamW showed improvement in many cases for transfer learning tasks…"
- glenn-jocher #3037 (2026-01-16): "lr_fit = 0.002 * 5 / (4 + nc) is an empirical heuristic we fit from internal sweeps to pick a safe starting lr0 … scaling the LR down as nc … grows." https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/pull/3037
- MuSGD: 8.4.0 #23176 → #23234 → #23350(Laughing-q, 2026-01-22) 현 규칙. 요약(봇): "shorter/typical trainings now default to AdamW, which may be more stable/effective out-of-the-box." 수치 없음. https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/pull/23350
- train 문서: "a hybrid optimizer that combines standard SGD updates with Muon-style orthogonalized updates. It is recommended for longer YOLO26 training runs and larger datasets." https://docs.ultralytics.com/modes/train/
- 불일치: #3360 "the optimizer switches to SGD for the remaining iterations"(2023-06-25) vs 같은 스레드 뒤 "The optimizer does not switch during training" — 코드 = 시작 때 한 번.
- ⚠️ 미확인: 10,000 문턱·lr_fit 식의 실험 자료("internal sweeps" 비공개).

## 5. 내장 튜너 model.tune
- 문서 https://docs.ultralytics.com/guides/hyperparameter-tuning/ : "a genetic algorithm that mutates and evaluates candidate configurations across many short training runs." · "iterations=40 with epochs=50 schedules 40 independent 50-epoch training runs." · "Iteration 1 … uses the default training hyperparameters as a baseline." · "tuning should be performed under settings similar to full training — including comparable datasets, epochs, and augmentations."
